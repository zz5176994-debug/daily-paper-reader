## 摘要
大型语言模型（LLM）智能体在交互环境中已展现出强大能力，但其从经验中持续演化的能力仍然有限。尽管微调能够实现适配，但微调对参数访问的依赖和高昂的计算成本限制了其灵活性，尤其对于大规模和闭源 LLM 而言。外部记忆提供了一种替代方案，使智能体无需修改模型参数即可积累经验。然而，现有方法主要关注经验的表示与组织，所获取的知识仍与特定任务和情境紧密耦合，限制了泛化能力。一个关键挑战在于如何将具体交互转化为抽象且可复用的知识，以超越单次经验并指导未来决策。为应对这一挑战，我们提出 SAGA（Self-evolving Agents through Experience-Grounded Abstraction，通过基于经验的抽象实现自我演化的智能体），一个用于 LLM 智能体的基于经验的知识抽象与利用框架。SAGA 逐步将交互轨迹转化为情景描述、可复用流程以及具有明确适用条件的原则，同时保持与执行证据的链接。检索到的原则被实例化为任务特定的指导，并通过纠正性反馈和重采样来优化候选动作。这形成了执行-抽象反馈回路，其中积累的知识指导未来交互，新经验不断更新层次化记忆。在 ScienceWorld 和 ALFWorld 上的实验表明任务性能得到提升，消融研究强调了上下文实例化和动作调节对于利用原则级知识的重要性。

## Abstract
Large language model (LLM) agents have demonstrated strong capabilities in interactive environments, yet their ability to continually evolve from experience remains limited. Although fine-tuning enables adaptation, its dependence on parameter access and high computational costs restrict its flexibility, especially for large-scale and closed-source LLMs. External memory offers an alternative by allowing agents to accumulate experience without modifying model parameters. However, existing methods mainly focus on experience representation and organization, while the acquired knowledge remains tightly coupled with specific tasks and contexts, limiting generalization. A key challenge is how to transform concrete interactions into abstract and reusable knowledge that guides future decisions beyond individual experiences.   To address this challenge, we propose SAGA (\underline{\textbf{S}}elf-evolving \underline{\textbf{A}}gents through Experience-\underline{\textbf{G}}rounded \underline{\textbf{A}}bstraction), a framework for experience-grounded knowledge abstraction and utilization in LLM agents. SAGA progressively transforms interaction trajectories into episodic descriptions, reusable procedures, and principles with explicit applicability conditions, while maintaining links to execution evidence. Retrieved principles are instantiated into task-specific guidance and used to refine candidate actions through corrective feedback and resampling. This creates an execution--abstraction feedback loop, where accumulated knowledge guides future interactions and new experiences continuously update hierarchical memory. Experiments on ScienceWorld and ALFWorld demonstrate improved task performance, with ablation studies highlighting the importance of contextual instantiation and action regulation for leveraging principle-level knowledge.

- SAGA 通过将交互轨迹抽象为**可复用的原则级知识**，并在任务中实例化和动作调节，有效提升了智能体在 ScienceWorld 和 ALFWorld 上的任务性能。

- **原则级动作调节**通过纠正性反馈和有限重采样，进一步提升了决策质量，与实例化形成互补。
- 执行—抽象反馈循环使智能体能够在多个轮次中持续改进，知识库不断更新，支持跨任务泛化（例如 ALFWorld 上对未见任务类型的改进）。

## 7. 优点

- **证据链接的层次抽象**：L0–L3 层层抽象且保留证据链，使原则可追溯、可审计。
- **知识利用机制创新**：不仅存储经验，还通过任务条件实例化和原则编译为谓词进行动作级调节，平衡了知识约束与生成策略的灵活性。
- **执行—抽象反馈闭环**：新经验不断更新记忆，实现持续自我演化，而非一次性优化。
- **多骨干、多范式验证**：在 prompt-based 和 training-based 两种范式下均验证了有效性，增强了结论的稳健性。

## 8. 不足与局限
